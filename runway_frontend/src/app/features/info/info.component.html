Returns

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love every piece you receive from RunWay. If for any reason you do not, we offer a straightforward 28-day return policy — no questions asked.

01

Initiate Return

Log in to your account, navigate to your orders, and select the item you wish to return. A return label will be emailed to you within 24 hours.

02

Package Your Return

Items must be returned in their original condition — unworn, with all tags attached, in original packaging. Footwear must include its original box.

03

Drop Off or Collect

Drop your parcel at any registered courier point, or schedule a complimentary collection from your address. We cover all return shipping costs.

04

Refund Processed

Once your return is received and inspected, your refund will be processed within 5 — 7 business days to your original payment method.

◈

Sale items and bespoke/made-to-order pieces are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.

Sustainability

Luxury and responsibility are not mutually exclusive. At RunWay, we believe the most sustainable garment is one made to last a lifetime — and we curate accordingly.

◈

Slow Curation

We do not add pieces seasonally. Each addition to our catalogue is deliberate and permanent, rejecting the cycle of fast consumption.

◈

Responsible Packaging

All RunWay packaging is made from recycled and FSC-certified materials. Our signature boxes are designed to be kept and reused.

◈

Carbon Offset

We offset 100% of our delivery emissions through verified reforestation projects in partnership with certified environmental organisations.

◈

Designer Standards

Every house on RunWay is vetted for ethical labour practices, material sourcing transparency, and responsible production volumes.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

At RunWay, your privacy is not a compliance checkbox — it is a commitment. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order or create an account. We also collect usage data (pages visited, time spent, device type) to improve the platform experience.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to fulfil your orders, communicate with you about your purchases, and improve our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and cart. We use analytics cookies (anonymised) to understand how the platform is used. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

Data Security

All data is transmitted over 256-bit SSL encryption. Payment information is processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment providers and is never stored on RunWay servers. We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also request a copy of all data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@runway.in. We will respond within 30 days.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. When we do, we will notify registered users by email and update the effective date below. Continued use of RunWay after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
